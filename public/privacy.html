Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026 · Mapica · mapica.app

This policy explains how Mapica (“we”) handles information when you use the Mapica iOS app and this website. Tickets, hotels, and car rentals are booked with independent partners; their own policies apply once you leave Mapica for checkout.

Who we are

Mapica is a travel product: locals plan itineraries, and travelers follow them in the app. Contact: hello@mapica.app · Support: support@mapica.app.

Information we collect

  • Account. Email, sign-in identifiers (Apple, Google), and profile details you choose to add (name, photo, city).
  • Trips. Destinations, dates, preferences, itineraries, messages with Locals, and photos you upload (for example a route cover).
  • Location. If you allow it, we use location to show walking directions and nearby context. You can refuse or turn it off in iOS Settings.
  • Device and usage. App version, language, crash logs, and basic analytics needed to run the service.
  • Payments for Mapica routes. If you buy a public route in Mapica, payment is processed by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers.

How we use it

We use this information to create and deliver trips, match you with Locals, operate the marketplace, improve the product, prevent abuse, and contact you about your bookings and account.

Partners and tickets

When you tap Buy ticket, Book, or Rent a car, Mapica opens a partner site or checkout (for example Tiqets, Viator, Expedia, DiscoverCars). Those companies receive the information needed for that booking (such as the attraction, dates, and city). They are independent controllers for that purchase. Mapica does not process ticket, hotel, or car-rental payments.

We also use infrastructure providers (hosting, maps, authentication, email) as processors under contract.

Sharing

We share trip details with the Local assigned to your request. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users and the service.

Retention

We keep account and trip data while your account is active and as needed for legal, tax, or dispute reasons. You can ask us to delete your account by emailing support@mapica.app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EEA/UK), you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of your data, and object to certain processing. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

Mapica is not directed at children under 16. Do not create an account if you are under that age.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above will change when we do. Material changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@mapica.app · Contact.
