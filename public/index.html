Tickets, stays, and cars

Mapica is an iPhone travel app. When a stop needs a ticket, a hotel, or a car, you book it in the app. Checkout is completed with our booking partners — not on this website.

Partners

We don’t take the payment

Ticket, lodging, and car-rental bookings are sold by partners such as Tiqets, Viator, Expedia, and DiscoverCars. They handle payment, confirmation, and support for those orders.
