Refund & Withdrawal Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026 · Seller: Mapica SAS · support@mapica.app

Digital products on Mapica (Ready Routes and Personal Trips) are sold by Mapica SAS. Local Creators author or perform the service; they are not the seller to the traveler.

Ready Routes (digital content)

Before purchase you must expressly consent to immediate access. Once access begins, you lose the 14-day right of withdrawal for that digital content, as permitted under applicable EU consumer rules.

Personal Trips (custom service)

Before purchase you must expressly request that Mapica begin creating your Personal Trip before the end of the 14-day withdrawal period. Once the service has been fully performed, you lose the right of withdrawal for that service.

Refunds

If something goes wrong with fulfillment, contact support@mapica.app with your order number. Eligible refunds are processed to the original payment method. Refund handling updates order status, creator payables, and (where required) Apple external purchase reporting.
